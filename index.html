Slow Time Pocket

Short description: Everything is falling faster and faster. Hold the screen to slow down time, then drag to slip between the blocks. How long can you keep up?

How to play

  • Drag your finger left or right to move the pocket watch.
  • Dodge the falling blocks, shards, and orbs. Touching one ends the run.
  • Hold anywhere on the screen to slow time. The slow-time meter drains while you hold.
  • Release to let the meter recharge. Time speeds back up when you let go.
  • Pass close to a block without touching it for a NEAR MISS bonus.

Controls

  • Touch: drag to move, hold to slow time.
  • Mouse: drag to move, hold the button to slow time.
  • Keyboard: Left and Right arrows to move, hold Space to slow time, Space to start or retry.
